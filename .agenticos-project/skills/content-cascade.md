# Skill: Content Cascade

Use to turn a single idea, topic, or piece of source content into a complete, platform-specific content system. Content Cascade does not produce one generic draft — it produces a coordinated set of assets that share a core insight but are independently optimized for each platform's format, algorithm, and audience behavior.

---

## When to Use This Skill

- You have a strong idea, insight, or piece of research and want to extract maximum distribution.
- You are building a content calendar and need a week's worth of assets from one topic.
- You want to repurpose a long-form asset (video, article, report) into short-form distribution.
- You need a complete multi-platform content launch, not a single post.

---

## Execution Instructions

### Phase 1 — Strategy (foundation for all assets)
Before producing any content:

1. **Define the core insight.** What is the single most important idea? If you removed everything else, what must survive?
2. **Define the audience.** Not a demographic — a specific person with a specific problem, belief, or goal. "Founders building in AI" is better than "tech people." "Founders building in AI who feel overwhelmed by tool choices" is better still.
3. **Define the promise.** What will the audience know, feel, or be able to do after consuming this content?
4. **Choose the angle.** What makes this perspective non-generic? Options:
   - Contrarian: challenge a widely-held belief.
   - Specificity: a precise insight most people do not share.
   - Transformation: before/after with a concrete mechanism.
   - Stakes: what happens if the audience ignores this.
5. **Produce 3 hook variants** across different frames (curiosity gap, contrarian, transformation, specificity, stakes). Test all three. Do not ship the first hook that works.

### Phase 2 — Long-Form Asset
6. YouTube script (1000–1800 words):
   - Hook (first 30 seconds): pattern interrupt + specific promise.
   - Body: 4–6 sections, each advancing the argument. Include a B-roll cue and on-screen text note per section.
   - CTA (final 60 seconds): one specific action, one line. Not "like and subscribe."
7. Blog post (1200–2000 words):
   - H1 with primary keyword. Meta description ≤ 160 characters.
   - H2 for each major section. Opening hook that matches the YouTube hook frame.
   - Internal link placeholders: `[INTERNAL LINK: topic]`.
   - Conclusion with one-paragraph summary and CTA.

### Phase 3 — Short-Form Repurposing
8. YouTube Shorts / TikTok (3 scripts, each ≤ 60 seconds):
   - Each script must stand alone — not a clip from the long-form video.
   - Hook in first 3 seconds. Loop potential. On-screen text for key stat or quote.
9. LinkedIn post (≤ 700 characters):
   - Hook line that does not get truncated at 140 characters.
   - 3–5 short paragraphs with line breaks.
   - End with a question to drive comments.
10. Instagram/LinkedIn carousel (10 slides):
    - Slide 1 = hook (must stop the scroll).
    - Slides 2–9 = one idea per slide, ≤ 40 words.
    - Slide 10 = CTA with one specific next step.
11. Newsletter section (150–250 words):
    - Subject line with A/B variant.
    - Preview text distinct from subject line.
    - Value-lead opening sentence. No "hope you're well."
    - Ends with a link or CTA.
12. Twitter/X thread (8–12 tweets):
    - Tweet 1 = standalone hook. Must work without the thread.
    - Each tweet adds one idea. No filler tweets.
    - Final tweet = CTA or summary with the core insight restated.

### Phase 4 — Quality Scoring
13. Score every asset on six dimensions (see format below).
14. Flag any asset scoring below 70 in any dimension with specific improvement notes.

### Phase 5 — Save
15. Save all assets to `/vault/content/[YYYY-MM-DD]-cascade-[topic-slug].md` as a single organized document.
16. Index with tags: `content`, `cascade`, `[platform names]`, `[topic keywords]`.

## Quality Standards

- Every asset must be independently usable — not a clip or excerpt that requires another asset for context.
- Hooks must be tested across at least 3 frames. The first hook is rarely the strongest.
- Platform format compliance is not optional. A LinkedIn post over 700 characters will be truncated. A Shorts script over 60 seconds will not fit the format. Check before finalizing.
- Quality scores must be honest. A 72/100 with useful notes is more valuable than a 91/100 that sends bad content to publish.
- Do not produce a generic cascade. If the angle is not specific enough to differentiate this content from any other post on the topic, revise the strategy before producing the assets.
